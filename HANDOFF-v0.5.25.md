# LifeGrid v0.5.25 handoff

This patch moves targeted capture to a fixed, normal-coordinate, noninteractive stage beneath the application stacking context. Targeted capture resolves independently from the annual publication/table and validates measurable dimensions. Rendering now requires a non-empty PNG result before opening Preview, while Download attaches a deterministic range-named anchor.

Next presets always resolve from LifeGrid's local today. Custom and relative targeted ranges may cross years up to 45 inclusive days. Events excluded with `showInExport: false` are removed before range, Category, Project, expansion, legend, and record-count processing. Empty targeted schedules render their date cells and an explicit empty-state message.

Unit coverage qualifies range resolution/validation and combined event predicates. Browser coverage seeds boundary, multi-day, category, project, private, empty, and cross-year records; it invokes `html-to-image`, checks Preview dimensions/data, and checks real Chromium PNG download bytes.

Manual mobile acceptance remains required for iPhone Share, long-press Save Image, rotation, and safe areas. AI interchange remains 4 and backup schema remains 7; there is no migration.
