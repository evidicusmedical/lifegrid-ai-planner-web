# Task status and triage contract — v0.5.22

`Task.status` is the authoritative workflow state. `triageStatus` remains an Advanced planning classification and cannot substitute for an explicit overall-status request. AI guidance maps blocked/in-progress/complete/to-do requests to `blocked`/`in-progress`/`done`/`todo`; explicit review, waiting, or scheduling requests may change only a supported triage value. Combined intent may preview both. Due-date clearing is `dueDate: null` and does not imply backlog or triage changes. AI-created work defaults to flat discrete titles in `<Workstream> - <Sub-area> - <Concrete action>` form; `projectId` stays separate and `parentTaskId` remains compatible but is used only on explicit request, with no deep hierarchy by default. AI deletion proposals are converted visibly to Blocked manual review with the required warning; Event deletion semantics are not changed.
